## 1.0.0

The first version of Coinroot: one calm place for every taka, whether it sits in a bank, in your wallet, on your phone or in savings that grow month by month.

- **Income, expenses and transfers**: moving money between your own accounts is never mistaken for spending.
- **Money tree**: follow any taka back to where it started, and see what's still left along the way.
- **Accounts** that know their own balance, with a quick way to match them to what you really have.
- **Monthly summary**: every month leaves a short story of what came in, what went out and what you set aside.
- **Backup and spreadsheets**: your records live on your phone, with a backup ready whenever you want one, and room to bring in what you kept elsewhere.
- **Optional account**: sign in only when you'd like your records on more than one phone; everything else works offline.
- Light and dark themes, and a Simple mode for the entries you make every day.
